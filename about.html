About Eldvik

Eldvik is a Nordic guild for World of Warcraft: Forever. We're adults from Sweden, Norway and Denmark who believe the journey matters more than the destination, that dungeons are better with people you like, and that nobody should feel dumb for asking a question. We raid, but we don't chase logs. Real life always comes first.

"The road is easier with someone walking beside you."

The Name

Eld is the Nordic word for fire; vik means bay or cove. Eldvik — a fire by the water, and a place to gather when the day is done.

What We Value

Respect First

However you play, whatever your experience — you'll be treated with respect from day one.

Show Up When You Can

Life comes first. We'd rather you log in when you're able than burn out trying to be somewhere every night.

Ask Anything

There's no such thing as a stupid question here. We were all new once.

No Drama, No Gatekeeping

No logs, no parses, no elitism. Just people enjoying a game together.
