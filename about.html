About Eldvik

Eldvik is a Nordic guild for World of Warcraft: Forever. Most of us are from Sweden, Norway, or Denmark. Our mindset is kind of that the goal isn't to hit 60 as fast as possible, have full BiS by week two, or get the best parses possible. Our goal is to have as much fun as possible with a great crew, where we as friends can run everything from dungeons and raids to PvP. It's totally okay if you don't know everything, no question is too dumb to ask. We also understand that life gets in the way sometimes and that's completely fine, we plan our events around our lives.

What you'll find here

Social

Community is a big reason why we created this guild. We want people to make long-lasting friendships, have fun together, and help each other out. That's the very core of the guild, and without it, the rest just doesn't work.

Group content (raids/dungeons)

We will do our absolute best in both dungeons and raids, but without it negatively affecting anyone. We don't want anyone to feel left out because of their performance or anything like that. We're all like-minded here, and all we want is for the bosses to go down, no matter how long it takes us. It's completely natural to make mistakes, and we learn from them.

PvP

For those interested (myself included), we'll also be doing PvP. Everything from open-world and battlegrounds to late-night duels. There's something for everyone!

No toxicity

We don't tolerate any toxicity here. Everything from sexism, racism, politics, religion and harassment stays outside our guild. We want to create a guild that people look forward to logging in and chatting with. Not feeling anxious about what the next message might contain.
