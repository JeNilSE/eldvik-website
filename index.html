A guild for Nordic players

Eldvik is a Nordic guild for World of Warcraft: Forever. Most of us are from Sweden, Norway, or Denmark. Our mindset is kind of that the goal isn't to hit 60 as fast as possible, have full BiS by week two, or get the best parses possible. Our goal is to have as much fun as possible with a great crew, where we as friends can run everything from dungeons and raids to PvP. It's totally okay if you don't know everything, no question is too dumb to ask. We also understand that life gets in the way sometimes and that's completely fine, we plan our events around our lives.
