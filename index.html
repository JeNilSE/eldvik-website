A Hearth for Nordic Adventurers

Eldvik is a Nordic guild for World of Warcraft: Forever. We're adults from Sweden, Norway and Denmark who believe the journey matters more than the destination, that dungeons are better with people you like, and that nobody should feel dumb for asking a question. We raid, but we don't chase logs. Real life always comes first.
