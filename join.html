Applying takes a few minutes. Here's how it works.

Apply to Eldvik

  1. Join our Discord

    This is where we talk, plan raids and hang out. Most of the guild is there most days.

  2. Go to #apply-to-eldvik

    Start a new post in the #apply-to-eldvik forum channel. It doesn't need to be long.

  3. Post a short introduction

    Say where you're from, what you like to play, and anything else you want us to know. An officer will reply.

It doesn't have to be long. A few sentences is enough.

Raid schedule will be announced as launch (November 4, 2026) gets closer. Join the Discord now to be part of the planning.
