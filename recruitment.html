Frequently asked questions

What ruleset and faction do you play?

PvP ruleset, and Alliance, though that's not fully confirmed yet.

Is there a server I need to be on?

No. World of Warcraft: Forever doesn't have separate realms, so there's nothing to pick and nowhere to transfer from.

How much do I need to play?

As much or as little as you like outside of raid nights. We don't track playtime. We're a grown up guild with jobs, studies and families.

Do I have to raid?

No. Plenty of members are here for the social side and don't raid at all.

Do I have to speak on voice chat?

No, but most people do during raids because it's easier to coordinate. Outside of raids it's optional.

Do I need previous experience?

No. We don't check logs or ask about experience.

What languages do you use?

Mostly English, since we're Swedish, Norwegian and Danish players together. Use whichever you're comfortable with.
