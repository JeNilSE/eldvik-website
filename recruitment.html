Recruitment

All classes and roles are welcome at Eldvik. We're social first, a Nordic guild from Sweden, Norway and Denmark, with no experience requirements. No logs, no parses, just good company and a willingness to show up.

Ruleset PvP
Faction Alliance (to be confirmed)
Raid Nights TBA

What we're looking for

Any class, any role

Play what you like.

Nordic at heart

We're a Nordic guild, Swedish, Norwegian and Danish. We speak English when someone doesn't follow along.

No experience needed

New to raiding, or coming back after a break? That's fine. We don't check logs or ask about experience.

How raiding will work

Raid nights will be announced closer to launch. Expect two evenings a week, European time, so weeknights work alongside jobs, studies and families.

Loot

Loot will use softreserve. Before each raid, you say which item you want, and if it drops, you get priority over anyone who didn't reserve it. Exact rules will be decided together with the guild before the first raid. The goal is something simple and fair, not a complicated points system.

Frequently asked questions

What ruleset and faction do you play?

PvP ruleset, and Alliance, though that's not fully confirmed yet.

Is there a server I need to be on?

No. World of Warcraft: Forever doesn't have separate realms, so there's nothing to pick and nowhere to transfer from.

How much do I need to play?

As much or as little as you like outside of raid nights. We don't track playtime. We're a grown up guild with jobs, studies and families.

Do I have to raid?

No. Plenty of members are here for the social side and don't raid at all.

Do I have to speak on voice chat?

No, but most people do during raids because it's easier to coordinate. Outside of raids it's optional.

Do I need previous experience?

No. We don't check logs or ask about experience.

What languages do you use?

Mostly English, since we're Swedish, Norwegian and Danish players together. Use whichever you're comfortable with.
