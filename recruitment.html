Recruitment

All classes and roles are welcome at Eldvik. We're social-first: adults from Sweden, Norway and Denmark — English speakers welcome too — with no experience requirements. No logs, no parses, just good company and a willingness to show up.

Realm TBA — not yet announced by Blizzard
Faction Alliance (to be confirmed)
Raid Nights TBA — two evenings a week, European time

What we're looking for

Any class, any role

We're not short on anything in particular. Play what you like — tank, healer or damage.

Adults, Nordic friendly

Adult players from Sweden, Norway and Denmark. English speakers are just as welcome.

No experience needed

New to raiding, or coming back after a break? That's fine. We don't check logs or ask about experience.

How raiding will work

Raid nights will be announced closer to launch — expect two evenings a week, European time, so weeknights work alongside jobs, studies and families.
